## Items needing your decision

1. **FY 2022 budget filename:** the opening letter describes the 2021 budget. Keep the original filename visible until you confirm the appropriate public title/year.
2. **2024 supplemental budgets:** the file labeled first has a September receipt; the file labeled second has a June receipt. Confirm sequence before presenting the ordinal labels as verified.
3. **Charter edition:** both duplicate files visibly say 2025 (1st Edition). The proposed public title follows the cover rather than the revision filename.
4. **FOI manual date:** the visible cover says May 2025, while its underlying text says October 2024. My earlier progress message called the extracted text the cover date; visual inspection corrected that.
5. **ISO letter:** describes a 2018 stage-one audit and recommendation for stage two. It must not imply current ISO certification.
6. **Financial/SALN labels:** several filenames imply a more specific document type than the first page supports. The conservative titles below reflect what was inspected.
7. **SALN 2019 deadline:** the memorandum contains an apparent date inconsistency. The proposed summary omits a deadline.
